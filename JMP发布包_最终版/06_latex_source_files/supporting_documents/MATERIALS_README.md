The title is generated from the shared manuscript for release v11. The letters contain no manually
maintained numerical results. They describe offline base planning, finite boundary refinement, the development comparisons, larger geometric case studies and five-program evaluation, and do
not claim hardware, cutting, independent training replication or hard-real-time evidence.

An anonymous main entry and separate title page are prepared options; current JMP anonymity and upload requirements have not been verified because the official guide returned HTTP 403. Highlights are prepared as an option, not certified as a current mandatory item.

guide calls for a manuscript Data Availability statement; acceptance of a separate
file must be resolved before actual submission. The statement covers the retained
materials supporting the reported results, without promising unavailable historical
training intermediates.

Author metadata, funding and contributions are preserved from the preceding package.
Authors must confirm the completed manuscript and supporting facts before submission. See `../author_checks.md` and the requirements record.
JMP and JIM are alternatives, not simultaneous submissions. Nothing has been submitted.
